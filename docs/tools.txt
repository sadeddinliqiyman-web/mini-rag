A chunk is a small piece of a larger document . Long documents are split into chunks because embedding models and language models work better with short texts, and because search becomes more precise when each piece convers one idea . Chunks often overlap a little so that no sentence loses its context. FAISS is a library made by Facebook AI Research. It stores embedding vectors and quickly finds the vectors most similar to query vector. In a RAG system, FAISS is used for semantic search over the chunks. Chroma is another popular vector database that can be used for the same purpose. An embedding is a list of numbers that represents the meaning of a text. Texts with similar meaning have embeddings that are close to each other.
